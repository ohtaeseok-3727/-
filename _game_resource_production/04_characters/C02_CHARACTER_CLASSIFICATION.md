## Barkan
Story Role: Iron-blood general running the fortress lure and canyon isolation operation.
Gameplay Role: battlefield-control boss; heavy front pressure while player disables war infrastructure.
Silhouette: larger than Common Orc, compact SmallKnight-compatible proportions, broad shoulders, heavy plate blocks, one command-standard/seal motif.
Equipment: heavy cleaver or poleblade, command seal, reinforced gauntlet/shoulder armor.
Signature Feature: iron-blood red command mark + heavy asymmetrical shoulder plate.
Required Animations: idle, walk, heavy attack, command signal, hit, armor-break/stagger, movement/retreat attempt, death.
Do Not: giant-monster anatomy, hyper-detailed armor, realistic proportions, magical glow as default.
